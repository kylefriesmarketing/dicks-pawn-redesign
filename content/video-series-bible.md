# "Behind the Counter" — Dick's Pawn Superstore video series

A repeatable short-form format for TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts.
One host, one counter, one numbered list, 45 seconds.

---

## 1. Why this format

The numbered-list-behind-the-counter video is the single most durable format in
local-business short form, and it works for three reasons:

1. **The number is a progress bar.** "Five myths" promises an end. Viewers stay
   for the countdown, which is why these clips hold retention past the 3s cliff.
2. **Myth-busting is argument bait.** Half the comments will be people insisting
   the myth is true. That is the algorithm's favourite signal.
3. **It costs nothing to repeat.** Same host, same counter, same lighting —
   only the list changes. Episode 12 is as cheap as episode 2.

The format is a *container*. The series bible below fills it.

## 2. The host — "Super Dick"

The About page already promises him: *"if you spot Super Dick around town —
that's our promise on the move."* The logo is a diamond cut into a hero shield.
The brand was already superhero-coded; this series just puts a face on it.

**Design decision: he is a man in a polo with a cape, not a costume.**

A foam mascot head cannot lip-sync, cannot hold an expression, and reads as
uncanny in AI video. A real guy working the counter who happens to be wearing a
cape is funnier, warmer, renders cleanly, and stays unmistakably *yours*.

| Axis | Locked value |
|---|---|
| Age / read | Late thirties, American, coastal-Carolina everyman |
| Build | Athletic-but-normal, well-proportioned |
| Hair | Short dark brown, neat, slightly tousled at the front |
| Face | Light stubble, faint laugh lines, natural skin texture |
| Top | Navy `#133564` short-sleeve work polo, collar open |
| Emblem | Small embroidered pale-blue diamond crest, left chest |
| Cape | Rich gold `#c9a24b` satin, fastened at the collarbones |
| Voice | Warm, dry-witted, conversational — never salesy |
| Accent | Neutral American. No forced drawl. |

**Persona sentence** — carry this verbatim into every character, board and clip
prompt or the performance drifts between episodes:

> A warm, dry-witted counter guy who has heard every myth a thousand times and
> enjoys knocking them down — easy-going, conversational, never salesy.

### Why gold and not red

Navy polo + red cape is Superman's palette, and the logo is already a shield.
Gold takes the series somewhere ownable: it ties to Dick's Bullion, it reads as
treasure, it catches shop light beautifully, and it keeps red free to mean one
thing only — the on-screen numbers and the CTA. Three colours, three jobs:

- **Navy** — the host, the base, the counter world
- **Gold** — the cape, the hero accent, the "we buy gold" throughline
- **Red** — numerals and calls to action, nothing else

## 3. The claim allowlist

**Nothing goes on camera that is not on this list.** Every line is sourced from
the company's own published pages. This is not legal caution for its own sake —
a pawn shop making a financial claim it cannot support is a real problem, and
"we pay the most in town" is exactly the kind of line that invites one.

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Family-owned, established 1987 | about.html |
| Five locations on the Grand Strand | about.html, locations.html |
| 4.9 stars, 2,000+ Google reviews | site header (verified on Google listing) |
| BBB A+ rated | about.html |
| Member, National Pawn Brokers Association | about.html |
| Pawn loans require no credit check | sell.html FAQ |
| Not repaying a pawn loan never affects your credit score | sell.html FAQ |
| Pawned items are stored securely and fully insured | sell.html FAQ |
| SC law requires government photo ID on all pawn and buy transactions | sell.html FAQ |
| Free, no-obligation appraisals | sell.html |
| Experienced jewelers on staff at every location, every day | services.html |
| Layaway available with no credit needed | services.html |
| 14-day returns, nationwide shipping | services.html |

**Off-limits on camera:**

- Dick's Title Loans. It is a legitimate service, but title lending is a
  regulated, high-risk financial product and short-form video is the wrong
  place for it. Keep it on the website.
- Interest rates, loan terms, APR, or any "better than a bank" comparison.
- Any specific payout figure, percentage, or "we pay X% of value."
- Invented staff history. Super Dick is a **host**, not a testimonial. He never
  says "in my fifteen years here" — he is presenting facts, not memories.

## 4. Episode scripts

Density is tuned to the render: ~30-35 spoken words per 15-second clip.
`CAPS` marks a volume spike. Em-dash marks a hard beat, not a pause.

### EP01 — 5 Myths About Pawn Shops  ✅ **PRODUCED** — `content/output/ep01-5-myths-FINAL.mp4`

> **Clip 1 · myths 1-2**
> Five myths about pawn shops. Number one — it's ALL stolen. South Carolina law:
> every transaction takes a government photo ID. Number two — it's all junk.
> We keep real jewelers on staff, every store.
>
> **Clip 2 · myths 3-4**
> Number three — pawning hurts your credit. It CAN'T. No credit check, and it
> never touches your score. Number four — your stuff disappears. It's stored
> safe and insured till you come get it.
>
> **Clip 3 · myth 5 + CTA**
> Number five — we're hoping you never pay it back. Wrong again. We'd rather see
> you walk out with your ring than keep it. Family-owned since nineteen
> eighty-seven. Five stores, Grand Strand.

### EP02 — Only One's Real  🔨 **IN PRODUCTION** — two chains, one fake, five tests

The first non-list episode, chosen from the research in
`content/research/ep02-viral-research.md`: a live authenticity test, the one pawn
format an AI host can perform that reliably clears a million views for real
shops. Stakes in the first line, five escalating tests that each fail to resolve
it, the reveal withheld to the last beat, cut on the last word. Prompts in
`content/prompts/ep02-*.md`. The acid beat — the hardest render — passed a
15-credit smoke test before anything else was spent.

> **Clip 1 · tests 1-2**
> Two chains. Only one's real. Five tests to find it. One — the magnet. Real
> gold ignores it. Both pass. Two — the weight. Gold is heavy. Hollow feels
> wrong. Still can't tell.
>
> **Clip 2 · tests 3-4**
> Under the glass. Three — the loupe. Clasp, wear, colour. Close. Not enough.
> Four — the acid. Scratch, drop, wait. Real gold sits there. Fake fizzes and
> fades. One of these is fading.
>
> **Clip 3 · test 5, the reveal, the invitation**
> Last test. Five — the jeweler. Real ones, every store, every day. Ten
> seconds. This one's real. This one's plated brass. Yours? Bring it in. Free
> look, no obligation. Five stores, Grand Strand.

### EP03 — 5 Questions We Get Every Single Day  — prompts written, unproduced.
Boards, clips and graphics spec in `content/prompts/ep03-*.md`.

Structurally this is a **relay, not a countdown**: clips 1 and 2 each end on a
question the next clip answers. That is a stronger retention device than EP01's
straight count, and it costs nothing — but it puts real load on the last beat of
clips 1 and 2, which must play as an unanswered question rather than a resolved
one. See `ep03-clips.md` rule 7.

> **Clip 1**
> Five questions, every day, same five. One — what's the difference between
> selling and pawning? Sell it, we keep it. Pawn it, we hold it and you come
> back. Two — do I need good credit?
>
> **Clip 2**
> You need NO credit. Pawn loans run on your item, not your score. Three — what
> do I bring? Your item and a government photo ID. That's South Carolina law,
> not our rule. Four — is my stuff safe?
>
> **Clip 3**
> Safe and insured, the whole time. Five — how do you decide what it's worth?
> Brand, model, condition, and what it actually resells for. Free look, no
> obligation. Five stores, Grand Strand.

## 5. Production pipeline

```
character (soul_2, 3:4, 2k)          -> ONE locked host image, reused forever
   |
board K (gpt_image_2, 21:9, 2k)      -> 8 vertical slots = 8 hard cuts
   |                                    K>1 chains the previous board for continuity
de-slop (seedream_v5_pro, 21:9, 2k)  -> strips the AI-plastic look, keeps composition
   |
clip K (seedance_2_5, 9:16, 1080p)   -> 15s, omni_reference, native speech
   |
concat (ffmpeg, stream copy)         -> final.mp4
   |
number burn (ffmpeg drawtext)        -> red numerals 1-5 on the beat
```

**Continuity rules that matter:**

- The host image is generated **once**. Every episode reuses the same
  `character_media_id`. Regenerating it is how a series loses its face.
- Wardrobe never changes between episodes. The cape is the constant.
- Board K>1 takes board K-1 as a reference image so the counter, the lighting
  and the guitar wall stay put across cuts.
- Text is **never** baked into generation. Numbers are burned in post, where
  they are crisp, on-brand, and editable.

Exact prompts live in `content/prompts/`.

## 6. Posting notes

- **Vertical 9:16, 1080p.** Same master works on all three platforms.
- **Caption carries an open loop.** The video answers five things; the caption
  asks a sixth. That is where the comments come from.
- **Pin a comment** that adds one real detail the video skipped.
- **Reply to the "it's all stolen" guy.** Every episode gets one. He is the
  best engagement you will ever get for free.
- Post EP01 and EP02 in the same week — myths then questions. The second one
  converts the people the first one argued with.
