## The eight rules, unchanged from EP03

1. `Hard cut to.` verbatim at the end of cuts 1-7. Seven markers, none after 8.
2. Static cuts say *locked-off, zero movement*. Never `handheld`, `drift`,
   `sway` or `micro-shake` inside a static cut.
3. Selfie cuts never mention the phone as an object. The camera **is** the phone.
4. The voice starts within 0.4s of frame one, and frame one is already mid-motion.
5. Two hands, always. Count the hand roles in every cut.
6. Clips 2 and 3 open mid-thought. No greeting, no re-introduction.
7. Clips 1 and 2 end on a **held, unresolved face** — here it is "still can't
   tell" rather than a question, but the mechanics are identical: brows up and
   staying up, lips closing, a beat of stillness. The clip cut is where viewers
   leave; an unresolved face is what carries them across it.
8. Every counting beat is TIGHT. Never wide, never macro.

## One rule this episode adds

9. **Chains are named by position, never by look.** "The chain lifted from the
   black tray" and "the chain on the bare glass" — every time, in every cut.
   Seedance cannot hold two identical objects apart by appearance across 24
   cuts; it can hold *where they came from*. If a render swaps them, cut or
   card that beat. Never re-roll for it.
