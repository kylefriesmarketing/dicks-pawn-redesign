## Title-loan verdict

**YES WITH GUARDRAILS**

Viable to post, not viable to perform, and only after the bible is amended. On the law (verified findings over raw): no platform bans organic title-loan content in an educational frame. YouTube's guidelines and monetization rules are silent on lending; Meta's Community Standards prohibit only advance-fee offers and 'guaranteed approval' without stating criteria; TikTok's current rules require the commercial-content toggle, and its revised guidelines effective TOMORROW (Sept 24, 2026) add 'high-risk financial services' (products that are 'predatory, contain extreme risks, or lack transparency') to the organic NOT ALLOWED list for 'trading, marketing, or providing access' while expressly ALLOWING 'financial education and information about financial products and services' — with zero enforcement history and 'predatory' undefined. The real on-camera constraint is federal: a short-form video is an 'advertisement' under Reg Z 1026.2(a)(2) (commercial message in any medium), so any stated rate must be an APR (1026.24(c)) and any payment amount, term/number of payments or finance-charge figure triggers full disclosures (1026.24(d)); SC enforces TILA as state law (37-3-301, 37-6-104) and bars false/misleading statements about rates, terms or conditions (37-3-304) and step-up teaser rates (37-3-413(7)). I took the refuters' side on three raw claims: the 'speed and volume' oral-ad standard is dwelling-only (use the general clear-and-conspicuous rule); Reg 15-60(j) on buy-out ads almost certainly does not apply (Dick's writes up to $10,000, above the $7,500 Consumer Finance Act cap, so it is a Title 37 supervised lender); and TikTok's US ad ban names 'Pawnbroker loans' and 'Payday loans', not title loans. None of that helps, because paid reach is closed anyway (Google: no US personal-loan ads at 36%+ APR or under 61 days, 'Auto title loans, Pawn shops' named; Meta: no loans repayable within 90 days, which catches a 30-day SC loan; TikTok Promote inherits the ad policy and a pawn-branded title ad will be read as a pawnbroker loan). On performance: there is no evidence anywhere of a lender or pawn account building an audience with title-loan content. TitleMax's TikTok has 87 followers and 59 likes over 18 videos; a Utah chain literally named 'Pawn Plus Half Off Title Loans' has 7 followers after 78 TikToks; DD Pawn Loans (116K YT subs) gets 20K on 'what happens if you can't pay' and 1.5K on the pitch; every 1M+ title-loan video is a borrower horror story or a '700% should be illegal' reaction, and the top comment on any title-loan video is 'what's the rate' or 'loan shark'. So a MrBeast-grade retention/share objective and a title-loan objective pull in opposite directions: MrBeast's 'more extreme the better' is exactly what Reg Z and 37-3-304 forbid, and the stakes in his sense (the car) cannot be gamified by a lender. Brand asymmetry is real: Jill told WPDE/WMBF in Aug 2025 'We're a pawn shop... We have overcome that', SC press is treating a Columbia title lender as blight in 2026, and 8.85% of SC max-rate filers post 300%+ APR, so the rate will be demanded in the comments. What the client can repeat to the owner: 'We can legally post an explainer that says what the site already says — no rates, no payments, no terms, no guarantees. It will be labeled AI on every platform, it cannot be boosted anywhere, it will attract the rate question and the usury crowd, and no lender has ever grown an audience with this content. Judge it on saves and DMs, never on views. The pawn side is where the reach is.' Finally, the on-camera ban was the consultant's own standing rule, never raised with the client (conversation.md line 230), so this is a client decision to reopen — amend bible §3 and START-HERE §5 with the scoped allowlist below; do not silently contradict them, because the packet tells any downstream builder to refuse it.

### Guardrails

- FRAMING: mechanics-only explainer voiced as 'here is how it works', never 'come get cash'. Host presents facts; he never says he took one, sold one, or has 'seen customers' (FTC fake-testimonial rule; Higgsfield's ugc-review-video gate will decline a title-loan promo — generate via generate_video with the repo's own prompt template, not the workflow).
- ZERO NUMBERS tied to money except a loan amount and minutes: no rate, no '%', no 'per month', no payment amount, no term ('30 days', '240 days', 'renew', 'six times', 'installments'), no finance-charge dollars, no intro/teaser rate. Loan amount ('up to $10,000 based on your vehicle's value') and 'as few as 30 minutes' are the only permitted figures (Reg Z comment 24(d)(1): loan amount is not a trigger).
- FORBIDDEN WORDS on camera, caption and pinned comment: 'guaranteed', 'guaranteed approval', 'approve everyone/anyone', 'refuse no one', 'regardless of credit' as a promise, 'no credit check' (drift toward guaranteed approval; Meta Community Standards violation with ad-account consequences), 'lowest rate', 'lowest rates in town', 'lowest rate guarantee', 'competitive rate', 'great rate', 'cheap', 'low interest', 'no interest', 'better than a bank', 'cash now/today' as the hook, 'limited time', 'largest pawn experience in the United States', 'over 29 years', 'late fees / collection' (the FAQ line conflicts with 37-3-413(2) and (5)), 'your car is never at risk', 'we'll pay off your other lender'.
- NEVER RECYCLE PAWN-LOAN CLAIMS into the title episode: '90 days', 'pay only the interest to extend', 'no credit reporting' come from a different statute and are Reg Z triggering terms for a title loan.
- REQUIRED PRESENCES: 'South Carolina residents only' spoken and on the card; 'ability to repay' language (37-3-413(3)); 'all credit types accepted' rather than 'no credit check'; 'as few as' before '30 minutes' every time; 'loan, not a sale' and 'we hold the title, it comes back when it's paid off' (both on dickstitleloans.com).
- REQUIRED ABSENCES in frame: no legible title document (a single blank folded white sheet stands in), no VIN, no plates, no real car, no key fob (fobs carry brand logos — plain metal keys on a plain ring), no readable text of any kind, no firearms, no other brand marks.
- CTA CARD: replace the series card (FREE APPRAISAL / 5 STORES - (843) 646-7166 / SHIPS ANYWHERE) — wrong on all three lines — with 'SC RESIDENTS ONLY / ALL CREDIT TYPES ACCEPTED / 1-843-663-AUTO'. Confirm the number with Jill first: dickstitleloans.com says 1-843-663-AUTO (boat/tractor pages say (843) 903-7296) while this repo's services.html routes 'Call to Apply' to the pawn line 843-646-7166. Never state a title-loan location count on camera: the site says 4 in most widgets, 5 in /howitworks and /about prose, and lists five addresses.
- NAME CHECK before it goes on a card: 37-3-503(7)(a) lets a supervised-loan licensee do business only under its licensed name — confirm 'Dick's Title Loans' matches the BOFI license.
- TWO BURNS FROM ONE RENDER: the phone number is never spoken, only burned. Reels/Shorts master carries the AUTO card. TikTok master carries no phone, URL or address in video, caption, bio-swap or pinned comment (the 2026 'trade' definition includes 'sharing contact or location information'); post it as financial education with the 'Your brand' commercial-content toggle ON. This is prudential, not a written prohibition, and the client should accept TikTok may label or throttle it.
- AI LABEL ON, every platform, regardless of realism: YouTube 'realistic scene that didn't occur' disclosure, Meta photorealistic-video disclosure, TikTok AI-generated label (auto via Content Credentials). A lender's video that looks real and wears an AI badge reads worse than one openly stylized, so the caption should own it ('our AI host Super Dick').
- NEVER BOOST. Not TikTok Promote, not a Reels boost, not a Shorts ad. Separately, check whether EP01/EP02 pawn-loan content is boostable on TikTok at all ('Pawnbroker loans' is a named US prohibition) before any paid spend.
- COMMENT DISCIPLINE: the top comment will be 'what's the rate'. Pre-write the pinned reply with no number: 'Rates and full terms are posted in every store and given in writing before you sign; ask for the APR.' Optional candid variant (client veto likely): 'The state makes us print THIS IS A HIGHER INTEREST LOAN on every agreement — read it before you sign.' Never reply with a figure, ever, including 'around' or 'as low as'.
- AMEND THE BIBLE: move Dick's Title Loans from off-limits to a scoped allowlist (the rows below), keep rates/terms/APR/payout banned, add 'SC residents only' and the AUTO card as mandatory for any title-loan episode, and record that the client asked for it on 2026-09-23.
- SUCCESS METRIC: saves, DMs and profile taps, plus YouTube Shorts 'Viewed vs. swiped away'. Views are not the KPI and no reach may be implied to the client (agreement: no guaranteed results).

### What would get the video or the account flagged

- Any spoken or burned rate not expressed as 'annual percentage rate', or any payment amount, term length, renewal count or interest-dollar figure without the full Reg Z 1026.24(d) block — a federal violation SCDCA can enforce under 37-3-304/37-6-104; a complaint route critics know.
- 'Guaranteed approval', 'everyone approved', 'approved regardless of credit' — a named Meta Community Standards violation (Fraud, Scams) whose enforcement menu includes restricting the advertising account, not just the post.
- A phone number, address, URL or 'link in bio' on the TikTok cut after Sept 24, 2026 — converts 'financial education' into 'marketing or providing access' to a possibly 'high-risk financial service'; undefined standard, no precedent, your account would be the test case.
- Posting it without the TikTok commercial-content toggle — FYF-ineligible / visibility reduced; repeated omissions escalate to posting restrictions or a ban.
- Boosting it anywhere: TikTok (pawnbroker/payday prohibition, licensing/18+ conditions), Meta (loans repayable in 90 days or less prohibited), Google/YouTube (36% APR ceiling, 61-day minimum, 'Auto title loans, Pawn shops' named; three strikes = account suspension).
- The AI host giving a testimonial or experience claim ('I got one last year', 'a customer of mine') — FTC fake-review/testimonial rule with civil penalties; also breaks the bible's host-not-testimonial lock.
- A teaser or 'first month' rate — 37-3-413(7).
- Legible text baked into a title document, plate or key fob — gibberish lettering that looks like a forged document on a lender's video; and a real brand logo on a fob.
- Reusing the series CTA card (5 STORES / 646-7166 / SHIPS ANYWHERE) or stating a title-loan store count — false statement about the lender's terms/conditions and an easy screenshot for a stitch.
- A duet that pairs the video with the mandatory 14-point 'THIS IS A HIGHER INTEREST LOAN... YOU ARE PLACING YOUR VEHICLE AT RISK' contract notice or an SC 550-850% APR filing — not a policy flag but the reputational one, and it attaches to a five-store brand with local-TV profile in the same market that covered the backpack giveaway.

## Key findings

- Title-loan content is legally postable organically on all three platforms in an educational frame (verified against the live policy text), but the binding constraint is Reg Z 1026.24: any rate must be an APR and any payment/term/finance-charge figure triggers full disclosures; SC enforces TILA as state law and bars misleading rate/term statements (37-3-304) and step-up teasers (37-3-413(7)). TikTok's guidelines effective Sept 24, 2026 add 'high-risk financial services' to the organic NOT ALLOWED list for marketing/providing access while expressly allowing 'financial education and information' — undefined 'predatory' standard, no enforcement history.
- There is no evidence any lender or pawn account has built an audience with title-loan content: TitleMax TikTok 87 followers/59 likes/18 videos; 'Pawn Plus Half Off Title Loans' 7 followers after 78 TikToks; DD Pawn Loans 20K on a warning-framed short vs 1.5K on the pitch; every 1M+ title-loan video is a borrower horror story or a 'should be illegal' reaction; comments under lender-POV content are 'loan shark'/'usury'/'what's the rate'.
- Paid rescue is closed: Google bars US personal-loan ads at 36%+ APR or under 61 days and names 'Auto title loans, Pawn shops'; Meta bars loans repayable within 90 days (a 30-day SC loan); TikTok bars 'Pawnbroker loans' and 'Payday loans' in the US, treats 'Loans' as allowed only with licensing/18+/disclaimers, and Promote inherits the ad policy. Check whether the existing pawn-loan episodes are boostable on TikTok at all.
- Pawn content is a mega-genre (MrBeast 491M, Dylan Ayres 41M, Pawn Reborn 66M, @pawn.man 1.2M followers) and the formats that reliably clear 1M for real shops are: a transaction with the number said out loud, a live authenticity test (fake Rolex 11.9M, acid test 582K), a warm named employee (Gem 'Erica' 1.7M), and an outsider testing the shop. Numbered lists and FAQ videos on the same accounts run 10-25x lower (29.6K vs 701K) unless each beat is a prop-plus-number mini-reveal or a mistake with stakes.
- EP01's own numbers: Higgsfield virality predictor (per 15s clip, free on this plan; it rejects anything over 16s) scores overall 45/46/46 with hook 23/23/22 out of 100; activation is flattest in seconds 1-3 of every clip (the 'Five myths' text-card open), the designed full-frame fact card and CTA card are the visual peaks, and the closer had dead air because it ran 3 words under budget. A new episode should be judged against these numbers on its raw clips before assembly.
- MrBeast, in his own words, transfers as reasoning, not recipe: the idea IS the thumbnail (ketchup, not front yard); title/cover set an expectation the first line must confirm; front-load ('no wasted words, short, concise, then tension'); stair-step so the payoff lands last; the pitch is content (CTA as item five); end abruptly; closed mouth beat open mouth in his own A/B. What does not transfer: 'the more extreme the better' (collides with Reg Z/37-3-304), wow-factor spend, 3/6-minute re-engagement (here the boredom points are the clip cuts at 15s/30s), A/B tooling (Shorts has none; use 'Viewed vs. swiped away'), and he said himself he has not cracked Shorts. The 'first 3 seconds' rule is TikTok's and Instagram's, not his.
- AI realism is decided in the first two seconds on skin, mouth and voice: viewers catch only 0.1% of deepfakes in tests but snap on waxy skin, lip-sync drift (45ms lead/125ms lag), a breathless metronomic voice, mechanical blinks and hands. The pipeline already carries the big fixes (de-slopped reference, cotton polo, deep-focus cluttered set, 8 hard cuts, 1080p omni_reference, no baked text); the remaining levers are prompt-level ('no music', a named shop sound, blink/glance/adaptor beats, one closed-mouth beat, hand-count law) and post-level (grain and softening after grade, no sharpening, a continuous room-tone bed under the concat). The AI label is unavoidable on every platform.
- The title-loan ban was the consultant's unilateral standing rule (conversation.md line 230), never raised with the client, so the tilt is a client decision to reopen — but the bible and START-HERE currently instruct any builder to refuse it and must be amended, not ignored.
- The lender's own copy is the only allowlist and it is internally inconsistent: '4 locations' in most widgets vs 'five' in /about and /howitworks vs five listed addresses; phone 1-843-663-AUTO on the site and (843) 903-7296 on boat/tractor pages, while this repo's services.html sends 'Call to Apply' to the pawn line 843-646-7166; 'over 29 years' (a 2016 figure); 'lowest rate guarantee' and 'refuse anyone' (unsubstantiated absolutes at odds with the 37-3-413(3) ability-to-repay duty); an FAQ line about 'late fees, additional charges, or even a collection' that conflicts with 37-3-413(2) and (5). The series CTA card is wrong on all three lines for a title-loan episode.

## MrBeast principles, applied to this format

- THE IDEA IS THE COVER. 'Five facts about X' is the front yard. Every concept below is built around one counterintuitive image you can see at 120px: keys kept while paper leaves; a ring that comes back; two chains and one is fake. If the image does not render, there is no episode — approve the hero frame before boards 2-3 or any 135-credit clip.
- EXPECTATION, THEN CONFIRMATION. The cover and the first spoken line make the same promise, and clip 1 visibly keeps it within 3 seconds. No text-card open: EP01's 'Five myths' card is the predictor's flattest 3 seconds. Open on the image already in motion (rule 4) and say the number after.
- FRONT-LOAD, NO WASTED WORDS. Hook = two declaratives, one concrete noun each, tension in the second ('This paper gets you cash. The keys? You keep those.'). Announce the list only after the image lands.
- STAIR-STEP TO THE PAYOFF. Order the five by rising counter-intuitiveness, not process order; the item that cashes the cover frame is number five. For a lender the escalation is in reassurance, never in what the viewer stands to lose.
- RE-ENGAGE WITH A GESTURE AT THE CLIP CUTS. The 15s and 30s boundaries are the 45-second equivalent of his 3- and 6-minute marks. Spend them on a physical, only-this-host beat (keys slid across the glass toward the lens; the fake fizzing) — never on a graphic. EP02's raised-and-held question (rule 7) already does this; keep it.
- THE PITCH IS CONTENT. The CTA is item five with a gesture, not an appendix. Burn the card over the final spoken line and cut within 0.5s of the last word. The series' 5.0s held closing card is the 'boring outro' he bans.
- DELIVER THE CLICKBAIT — HERE THAT IS A LEGAL FLOOR. Every claim on the cover and in the hook is on the allowlist; for the title episode 'never lie' is Reg Z and 37-3-304, not just audience trust.
- COVER: ONE FACE, ONE IDEA, CLOSED MOUTH. His own A/B: mouth closed raised watch time on every video. Face big and off-centre left, one two-to-four-word line, under 50 characters in the title, no show-branding ('| Dick's Pawn | #Shorts').
- TEST, DON'T GUESS — AT LOCAL SCALE. No thumbnail A/B exists on TikTok/Reels/Shorts. Substitute: run virality_predictor on every raw 15s clip against EP01's baseline (hook 22-23) before assembling; post two Shorts a week apart that differ only in the first 3 seconds and read 'Viewed vs. swiped away'.
- MORE DESIGNED FULL-FRAME BEATS. The predictor says EP01's fact card and CTA card were the visual peaks. Plan one full-bleed card per episode as a designed beat over a claim worth hearing, not as a patch — and use the same technique as the free fallback when a prop beat renders wrong.
- WHAT DOES NOT TRANSFER, SAY SO TO THE CLIENT: extreme claims, visible spend, hired talent and crew (that is the $10k tier), and 'MrBeast's Shorts formula' — he has said he has not cracked Shorts; what we are applying is his reasoning.

## AI-realism playbook

- VOICE — THE CALL: keep Seedance 2.5 native speech for EP02 and do NOT run voice_change on it. The host's voice is now part of the locked identity (EP01 is out); a swapped or cloned voice on EP02 is a face change by another name, native audio is within ~1 frame of lip-sync at clip start, and the 8 hard cuts reset any drift every ~2s. Where I disagree with the research: do not record the owner or a staffer as the audio reference — a real person's voice on a synthetic face is a disclosure and consent headache and breaks 'host, not staff'. Instead, from EP03 on, test Seedance's audio_references with 5-10s of Super Dick's OWN EP01 track (clean mono WAV, 44.1/48k, peaks -6 to -3 dBFS, no room tone, no leading silence) tagged 'accent and vocal delivery reference only' so the voice stops drifting between episodes; run it as a 5-second 480p smoke test first. Never make a separate TTS pass.
- FACE: nothing downstream adds texture the reference lacks. Keep the soul_2 → de-slop chain; confirm the LOCKED host still shows pores, stubble, laugh lines and muted catchlights (no oversized iris speculars). Keep 'pore-level skin with no smoothing' and 'no beauty filter' in every clip prompt; never sharpen in post.
- MOUTH: one closed-mouth beat per clip (lips together, no voice, between chunks); voice within 0.4s of frame one with no leading silence; keep 30-35 words (the bible) rather than the research's 20-25 — EP01 held sync at that density and its under-dense closer produced dead air; never respell or stretch vowels, max 1-2 CAPS words per line, commas for rhythm; QA 2-3 mid-word frames per clip for doubled lip edges and the single-white-block teeth.
- EYES: write blinks and glance-breaks as beats ('blinks once mid-word', 'recovered eye-flick', 'glance to the case and back'); no unbroken eye-contact hold longer than ~4s; a Duchenne half-smile on the closer, not a grin.
- HANDS: hand-count law — name each hand's role in every cut and park the idle one on the glass or off-frame; any two-hand prop action (keys + paper, loupe + ring, magnet + chain) goes in a STATIC cut; no hand crossing the face; props are plain (no fob, no label, no stamp); count hands in every cut including frame edges before submit.
- MOTION/CAMERA: eight visually distinct hard cuts (adjacent slots differ in POV and distance band), 'Hard cut to.' on cuts 1-7, no motion words inside static cuts, at most one deliberate move per cut and never on every cut, one candid phone device per CLIP (autofocus hunt or pinch-zoom overshoot-and-correct), keep the host alone in frame (Seedance's own weak spot is multi-subject interaction).
- BACKGROUND: chain EP02 board 1 from EP01 board 1 (already the rule) and keep the four real store photos as image references; deep focus, cluttered, lived-in, zero legible text; keep the guitar-headstock sentence in the positive.
- AUDIO PROMPT: add the literal phrase 'no music' and one named off-frame shop sound per clip ('door chime once, distant register drawer') to the existing 'iPhone microphone audio with natural room tone' line — the main Seedance audio artifact is unrequested music.
- AUDIO POST: lay a continuous low-level shop-ambience bed under the whole master (per-clip loudnorm + concat leaves the room tone stepping at every clip boundary); gentle dynamic de-ess only in 3-6 kHz with a touch of HF roll-off to tame the 'metallic s'; never over-de-ess (softened consonants are their own tell); keep -14 LUFS per clip before concat.
- PICTURE POST (order matters): if upscaling at all, upscale FIRST with low denoise (try Higgsfield upscale_video 'ugc' preset at 1080p/24-30fps against the raw 1080p render — the raw may read more real; never 60fps), then grade toward the real store photos, then 2-3% grain (or 20-40% overlay on Soft Light), then a final 0.3-0.8px softening. Keep 'no film grain, no cinematic grade' in the prompt — generated grain is uniform; real grain goes on last.
- DURATION/QA: 15s clips only (never extend); triage per START-HERE §8 (transcribe → scene cuts → cut or replace picture → re-render last); run virality_predictor on each raw clip (free, ≤16s) and refuse to assemble if the hook does not beat EP01's 22-23; keep the clean master in git.
- DISCLOSURE: the AI label goes on every post regardless of how real it looks; lean into it in the caption rather than hoping it passes.
